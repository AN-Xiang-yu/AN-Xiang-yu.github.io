For parents · 3 steps

Twenty minutes on the sofa, one good conversation

  1. 01

    Watch together

    Pick a theme and press play. English subtitles are on by default.

  2. 02

    Pause on a moment

    Stop when a story lands. Ask what your child noticed.

  3. 03

    Talk at dinner

    Use the questions on the video page. There are no wrong answers.
