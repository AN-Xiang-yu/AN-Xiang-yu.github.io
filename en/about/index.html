01 · The library

What cnimee is

cnimee (创新道德教育) is a library of Chinese talks on virtue, family life and honest work, made for parents and teachers.

The Chinese site holds the whole library. This English edition is a curated selection, with English subtitles and short notes so the cultural background is not lost.

Watch without being watched

We count visits. We do not follow people.

We use Umami, a statistics tool we run ourselves. It counts page views, not people, and builds no profile of you.
