01 · The library

What cnimee is

cnimee (创新道德教育) is a library of Chinese moral education talks: caring for parents, civic and professional ethics, cause and effect, and personal stories of change. The talks were given at schools, communities, workplaces, prisons and drug rehabilitation centres.

The Chinese site holds the whole library. This English edition is a curated selection, with English subtitles and short notes so the cultural background is not lost.

04 · Your data

Privacy & contact

Watch without being watched

For now this site runs no visitor statistics, and it does not follow people.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, processes access logs (such as IP addresses and browser details). We do not see those logs ourselves.

  • No YouTube contact before you press play
  • No ad tracking
  • Progress stays on your device
  • No account needed

STATISTICS · NONE · HOST · GITHUB PAGES

Videos play through YouTube. Opening a page does not contact YouTube; only after you press play does YouTube (Google) see your IP address and handle data under its own rules. Your text size and viewing progress stay in your own browser’s local storage and are never uploaded.
